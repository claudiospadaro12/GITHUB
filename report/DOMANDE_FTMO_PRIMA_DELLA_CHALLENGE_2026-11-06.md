## Bozza di messaggio al supporto (in inglese, da incollare)
> Hello FTMO team,
> before buying more than one Challenge I would like to confirm a few points in writing.
> 1. I trade with automated Expert Advisors (EAs) from third-party vendors, on gold and forex, with pending orders and open positions held for hours/days. Are these EAs allowed on Challenge and on funded accounts? Are there restrictions on grid-like logic, pending orders, hedging, trade frequency or minimum trade duration?
> 2. I plan to open several accounts with the same or partly the same EAs and similar settings. What is the maximum total capital allowed across my accounts? Is it counted in USD for accounts in EUR, across all accounts or only active ones, and how do you define "identical or substantially similar" trading? What happens if I exceed it?
> 3. Does the Scaling Plan change this maximum?
> 4. How exactly are the Maximum Daily Loss and Maximum Loss calculated (balance or equity, floating, commissions and swaps), and at what time does the daily limit reset?
> 5. Are there restrictions around high-impact news on Challenge and funded accounts? Is a VPS allowed, and are EAs that connect to the vendor's licensing website through WebRequest allowed?
> 6. Which account sizes are currently available in EUR (is there a 160,000 account)?
> Thank you. Please reply in writing so I can keep the confirmation.

## Come usare le risposte
Ogni risposta si incolla nella colonna "Risposta" con la data e la fonte (pagina o email). Una domanda senza risposta scritta resta "NON VERIFICATO" e non si compra finché non è chiusa. Io aggiorno il piano delle combinazioni e il tetto di capitale con le risposte.
