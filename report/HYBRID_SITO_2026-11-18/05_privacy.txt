PRIVACY POLICY — Hybrid Finance (A trademark operated by PrimeFuture Consulting LLC)

PrimeFuture Consulting LLC, 16192 Coastal Highway, Lewes, DE 19958, USA (Delaware File Number 3402639), email info@hybridfinanceinternational.com, is the controller of the personal data processed through the website, landing pages, forms, calls, messaging channels and services offered under the Hybrid Finance trademark. This Policy is drafted in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws. No Data Protection Officer has been appointed.

2. DATA WE PROCESS
- Data you provide: name, email address, phone number, and the information you give in forms, surveys, calls or chats (such as trading experience, objectives, services previously used, financial availability and contact preferences); testimonials, interviews or feedback you choose to share.
- Browsing and usage data: IP address, device and browser, pages visited and interactions, and data collected by tracking tools (Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, Google Ads, Microsoft Clarity), as described in the Cookie Policy.
- Data from the platforms used to operate the Service (CRM, booking, messaging and advertising platforms).
- Payment data: where a payment to Hybrid takes place, it is handled by external payment providers acting as independent controllers under their own privacy policies; Hybrid does not store full card details.
- Calls and automated assistance: commercial and support calls may be transcribed with automated tools, including AI services provided by third parties (such as Google), to create call notes for assistance and quality purposes. The audio is not stored: only the transcript and the resulting notes are kept. On messaging channels (such as WhatsApp) you may interact with an automated virtual assistant, which is identified as such; those conversations are processed for the same purposes.

3. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES
a) Handling your requests, booking and holding calls, providing assistance – pre-contractual measures taken at your request.
b) Delivering the services, managing the relationship, access, renewals and requests – performance of a contract.
c) Marketing communications by email, SMS, WhatsApp and phone, offers, remarketing and personalised advertising – your consent (for example the consent you give when submitting our forms) and, within the limits allowed by law, our legitimate interest.
d) Publishing testimonials and feedback you voluntarily provide – your consent, expressed by sharing them in the knowledge that they may be published; you may ask for removal at any time.
e) Call transcription, automated assistance, analysis of aggregated data, improvement of services and content, security – our legitimate interest.
f) Compliance with legal, tax and accounting obligations, prevention of fraud and abuse, establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims – legal obligation and legitimate interest.
Providing the data marked as required is necessary to answer your request or deliver the service; marketing consent is optional.

4. RECIPIENTS AND TRANSFERS OUTSIDE THE EEA
Data may be accessed by authorised staff and collaborators, and by providers acting as processors: CRM and funnel platforms, hosting, email and messaging providers, call transcription and AI tools, analytics and advertising platforms, payment providers (as independent controllers), and competent authorities where required by law. Data is not sold.
Because the controller is established in the United States and uses US-based providers (such as Meta, Google and CRM platforms), data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. Transfers take place in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR: adequacy decisions (including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients), Standard Contractual Clauses or other lawful safeguards.

5. RETENTION
Contact and marketing data: until you withdraw consent or ask for deletion. Service data: for the duration of the relationship and then for the period required by legal, tax and accounting obligations. Call transcripts, call notes and chat logs (call audio is not stored): for the time needed for assistance and quality purposes and, where relevant, to defend legal claims. Data relevant to disputes: for the time needed to establish, exercise or defend rights. Data is then deleted or anonymised.

6. YOUR RIGHTS
You may at any time request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent (without affecting prior processing) by writing to info@hybridfinanceinternational.com. You may unsubscribe from emails through the link in each message and ask us to stop WhatsApp, SMS or phone contact. You may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your country of residence (for example the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali in Italy).

7. MINORS
The services are intended for adults (18+). Data of minors, if collected, is deleted as soon as possible.

8. COOKIES AND SECURITY
Cookies and similar tools are described in the Cookie Policy. Hybrid adopts appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data; no Internet system can guarantee absolute security.
