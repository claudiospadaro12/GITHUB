1- Yes, you can. If you plan to use trading robots (Expert Advisors - EAs), keep in mind that if you use a third-party EA, others may already be using the same EA and therefore the same strategy. Using a third-party EA may result in your FTMO Account being denied if you exceed our Maximum Capital Allocation. For more details, you can visit this link:
https://ftmo.com/en/faq/which-instruments-can-i-trade-and-what-strategies-am-i-allowed-to-use/.

2- The Maximum Capital Allocation is enforced "per trader or strategy". If you use a third-party EA or signal which other traders are also using, your accounts are evaluated alongside theirs; if the combined total of accounts running the identical strategy exceeds $400,000, it can trigger a breach of the Max Capital Allocation rule. If you run genuinely different strategies or EAs across accounts, they are evaluated per strategy. Each distinct strategy has its own $400,000 limit, provided they are not identical or highly correlated strategies intended to circumvent the cap. FTMO Challenges and FTMO Accounts count towards the Maximum Capital Allocation rule. Once you fail the Account, it is no longer taken into consideration.

4- You may find our pricing for each FTMO Challenge here: https://trader.ftmo.com/start-challenge.

5- FTMO offers a maximum leverage based on your account type:
- Standard Account: Up to 1:100.
- Swing Account: Up to 1:30 (Available only on the 2-Step FTMO Challenge).

How Margin Works: To open and maintain a trade, a certain amount of funds (Margin) must be available. The higher the leverage, the smaller the margin required.

Leverage & Margin Examples:
- Forex (Majors/Minors): 100% Margin Percentage. Standard: 1:100 | Swing: 1:30.
- Forex (Exotics): 150% Margin Percentage. Standard: 1:66.67 | Swing: 1:20. (Applies to all Exotics except USD/SEK).
- Metals, Cash Oil & Major Indices: 200% Margin Percentage. Standard: 1:50 | Swing: 1:15.
- Minor Indices & Commodities: 333% Margin Percentage. Standard: 1:30 | Swing: 1:9.
- Crypto, Equities & Agriculture: 3000% Margin Percentage. Standard: 1:3.33 | Swing: 1:1.

Please ensure to check the specific margin requirements for each instrument on your platform or visit our Symbols page: https://ftmo.com/en/symbols/.

6- On a Standard FTMO Account or FTMO Instant Account, you must close your positions when the market is closed for more than 2 hours. In most cases, this means you can keep a trade open until the market is closed for more than 2 hours (e.g. the weekend, or overnight for some instruments). Trading hours vary by instrument, so check the platform, our Symbols page, or Trading Updates.

This restriction doesn't apply to the Swing account type. Unlike the Standard FTMO Account, the Swing account type has no restrictions on news trading or on holding trades over the weekend. It is available only through our 2-Step Evaluation Process.

7- All type of Orders are taken into consideration for the server requests.

8- We allow hedging within the same trading account on our platform. This means you are free to buy and sell the same instrument within a single account as part of your risk management strategy. There are no restrictions from our side regarding hedging in this manner, and we support our traders in employing such strategies to protect their trades.

Restrictions on Cross-Account Hedging: However, it is important to note that while hedging within a single account is permitted, hedging across multiple trading accounts is strictly prohibited. This includes any form of trading that involves opening opposite positions irrespective of the entity/company (prop firms/brokers). Such practices bypass the purpose of the Evaluation Process, where we look for genuine trading talents, and therefore are considered a violation of our FTMO Challenge Terms and Conditions and the FTMO Account Terms and Conditions.

Violations of our FTMO Challenge Terms and Conditions and the FTMO Account Terms and Conditions, including the prohibition of cross-account hedging, can result in account terminations or other necessary actions.

9- Gap trading is a high-risk practice that can lead to unfavourable outcomes in real market conditions because of increased volatility. It refers specifically to intentionally executing positions right before a market closes to gamble on or exploit the expected price "gap" when the market reopens, rather than maintaining a genuine, long-term trade.

This practice is explained in detail on our Forbidden Trading Practices page, here: https://ftmo.com/en/forbidden-trading-practices/ (clause e (i, ii)).

Since FTMO is looking for serious traders who have an edge, gap trading is the exact opposite.
